COMMONSLENS

Everything you can do with CommonsLens

CommonsLens is a visual research workspace for discovering Wikimedia Commons media, checking its context and reuse information, organizing files, and exporting the metadata you need.

Free, open-source Wikimedia research tools

CommonsLens runs in the browser, requires no Commons account, and uses official Wikimedia and Wikidata APIs. Media remains hosted by Wikimedia Commons and retains its original source and rights information.
